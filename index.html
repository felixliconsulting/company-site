How everything works together

A great funnel doesn’t stop when someone fills out a form. It continues through every interaction afterward.

  1. 01 A visitor downloads your guide.
  2. 02 They immediately receive a welcome email.
  3. 03 Automated emails introduce your business over the next few days.
  4. 04 When ready, they book a consultation.
  5. 05 Your CRM creates their contact automatically.
  6. 06 Your calendar confirms—and your team gets notified.
  7. 07 After they buy, onboarding emails send themselves.
  • No leads forgotten
  • No manual copying between tools
  • No wondering who needs a follow-up

No. If you already have tools you’re happy with, we’ll work with them whenever possible. If not, we’ll recommend solutions that fit your business rather than forcing unnecessary complexity.
