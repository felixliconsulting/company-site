How Everything Works Together

A great funnel doesn’t stop when someone fills out a form.

It continues through every interaction afterward.

Imagine this:

  1. A visitor downloads your guide.
  2. They immediately receive a welcome email.
  3. Over the next few days, they’re introduced to your business through automated emails.
  4. When they’re ready, they book a consultation.
  5. Your CRM automatically creates their contact.
  6. Your calendar confirms the appointment.
  7. Your team receives a notification.
  8. After becoming a customer, onboarding emails are sent automatically.

Everyone knows what happens next.

  • No leads forgotten.
  • No manual copying between software.
  • No wondering who needs a follow-up.

That’s what happens when your funnel and automations work together.
