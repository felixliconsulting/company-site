How everything works together

The funnel shouldn’t end when someone fills out a form. It should keep going through every step after.

  1. 01 A visitor downloads your guide.
  2. 02 They get a welcome email right away.
  3. 03 A short email sequence introduces your business over the next few days.
  4. 04 When they’re ready, they book a consultation.
  5. 05 Your CRM creates their contact automatically.
  6. 06 The calendar confirms, and your team gets notified.
  7. 07 After they buy, onboarding emails go out on their own.
  • Leads don’t get lost
  • No copying data between tools by hand
  • You always know who needs a follow-up
